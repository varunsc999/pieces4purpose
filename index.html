About Pieces4Purpose

A nonprofit built on the belief that every kid has a purpose.

Pieces4Purpose is a nonprofit organization with a goal to teach the younger community about engineering and technology. We believe that hands-on, competitive learning is one of the most powerful ways to spark a lifelong interest in STEM.

Through the FIRST LEGO League program, our team of middle schoolers designs and programs autonomous robots, researches real-world problems, and develops the teamwork and communication skills that matter far beyond the competition floor.

The Program

What is FIRST LEGO League?

FIRST LEGO League (FLL) is a global STEM education program that introduces students in middle school to robotics, engineering, and real-world problem solving through hands-on learning with LEGO. Founded by the nonprofit FIRST, the program blends creativity, teamwork, and technology to inspire young minds.

Every season, FLL releases a new challenge. Teams design an autonomous robot, research a real-world problem, and present their findings to a panel of judges, all while demonstrating the Core Values that make the program special.

What you'll learn

FLL is more than building with LEGOs. You'll develop real technical and professional skills that last a lifetime.

Engineering

Design and build a LEGO robot capable of completing complex autonomous missions on a competition field.

Coding

Program your robot using block-based or Python code to navigate, sense, and react to the playing field.

Research

Identify a real-world problem, develop an innovative solution, and present it to expert judges.

Teamwork

Collaborate, communicate, and compete together, growing as individuals and as a unit.

Our Mission

Every piece matters. Every kid has a purpose.

Pieces4Purpose is a nonprofit organization with a goal to teach the younger community about engineering and technology. We use the excitement of competitive robotics to show middle schoolers what they're truly capable of.

Our Story

Pieces4Purpose was founded because middle school is a critical time when kids begin deciding what they want to do in life. By this time, many children will find an interest in robotics or building. This team is the way for them to develop that interest into skills they can use in life. We use the FIRST LEGO League program as the hook because it's a globally respected competition that demands real engineering, real research, and real teamwork.

Today, Pieces4Purpose is actively recruiting for the 2025-2026 season and preparing for regional competition. Every member of this team is a volunteer, and every dollar raised goes directly to supplies and registration.

Our Three Pillars

Our main focus is development. Students leave not just with technical skills, but with confidence and character.

Technical Excellence

We don't build the robots for the students. We teach mechanical design, logic, and programming so that they can build it themselves.

Innovative Thinking

The Innovation Project requires students to research real-world issues, invent original solutions, and pitch them to experts.

Gracious Professionalism

Compete fiercely, but treat everyone with respect. Help other teams, and value learning over winning.

For Parents and Newcomers

What is FIRST LEGO League?

FIRST LEGO League (FLL) is a global STEM education program that introduces students in middle school to robotics, engineering, and real-world problem solving through hands-on learning with LEGO. Founded by the nonprofit FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology), the program blends creativity, teamwork, and technology to inspire young minds.

The Three Parts of the Challenge

Every season, FIRST releases a new theme based on a real-world topic. Teams of up to 10 students spend months preparing for a regional tournament and are judged in three categories:

Robot Game Innovation Project Core Values
Part 1

The Robot Game

Teams design, build, and program an autonomous LEGO robot using Mindstorms or SPIKE Prime to complete missions on a 4 by 8 foot playing field. In 2.5 minutes, the robot must score as many points as possible without human control.

  • Mechanical design and structural engineering
  • Block-based or Python programming
  • Strategy and competition planning

The LEGO SPIKE Prime kit: what the team uses for building and programming robots

Part 2

The Innovation Project

Teams research a real-world problem related to the season's theme, design a new solution, and share it with professionals. At the competition, they present it to a panel of judges in 5 minutes.

  • Research and expert interviews
  • Public speaking and presentation
  • Creative and critical thinking
Part 3

Core Values

The heart of the program. Judges evaluate how the team works together, handles disagreements, and embodies the FIRST philosophies of Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, and Fun.

  • Gracious Professionalism
  • Coopertition (helping competitors)
  • Inclusivity and respect
